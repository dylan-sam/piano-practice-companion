## Piano Practice Companion 1.1

### What's new

**A new menu.** The main menu has three areas: **Play along**, **Ear Training** and **Sightreading**, each with its own screens.

**Play along**
- **Lead Sheet Player**: melody and chords on a real lead sheet, played with the band. 1,479 public domain lead sheets are included (OpenEWLD and the Nottingham folk tunes), and you can import your own MuseScore files (MusicXML).
- **Chord Player**: the accompaniment player you know. **Pattern Creator** (formerly Style lab) lets you make your own groove; **Edit Song** edits a chart.
- **Export** now offers four clear choices: the songs you wrote or edited, the songs shared with you via link, the imported ones, or all three. The song list shows each playlist under its own heading.
- Swung styles show the swing marking at the top of the sheet; sixteenth notes always stay even.

**Ear Training**
- **Melodic Dictation** (new): hear a few bars of a song or a lick, then write the hidden notes on the staff, as in a score editor: tap to place a note, tap again or drag to move it, add sharps and flats. The rhythm is written for you. Choose how many notes to guess, songs or licks, with or without alterations, chords shown or hidden. Hear the tonic or a cadence first.
- **Name the progression**: "Focus on mistakes" is now written in full on phones.

**Sightreading** (with a MIDI keyboard)
- **Lead Sheet Sightreading**: play a lead sheet's melody with the band, note by note judged and scored.
- **Exercises Sightreading**: triads, seventh chords, guide tones, scales, approach notes and licks.
- **Lick Sightreading**: one lick played over every chord of a song or of an endless line of progressions, transposed at each chord change. A database of 1,470 licks and exercises is included.
- **Chord Sightreading**: import your own charts here too.
- The **Scoring** button (bottom bar) sets how notes are judged: timing window, any octave, guide melody.

**Smoother playback everywhere**
- Changing the style, piano pattern, choruses, guide melody, any octave or the band never restarts the song and no longer makes a click: the band goes on from where it is.
- Practice keys and speed-up apply without starting over.
- Endless lines go from one sheet to the next without a gap or a noise, and start each new sheet with a bar of rest to look back up.
- While an endless line plays, *Pick a song* and *Random song* only open their menu.

### How to get it

| Device | What to do |
|---|---|
| Computer, tablet or phone | open the [web demo](https://dylan-sam.github.io/piano-practice-companion/) in a browser: nothing to install |
| Android phone (Android 7.0 or newer) | download **piano-practice-companion-debug.apk** below, open it, allow the install when Android asks, then tap **Install** |

Already have 1.0 on Android? Install this APK over it; your songs and settings are kept. If Android refuses, uninstall the old version first (use **Export → All 3** before, as uninstalling erases your saved songs).
