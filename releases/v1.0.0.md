## Piano Practice Companion 1.0.0

The first public version.

### How to get it

| Device | What to do |
|---|---|
| Computer, tablet or phone | open the [web demo](https://dylan-sam.github.io/piano-practice-companion/) in a browser: nothing to install |
| Android phone (Android 7.0 or newer) | download **piano-practice-companion-debug.apk** below, open it, allow the install when Android asks, then tap **Install** |

Android may say the app comes from an unknown developer: this is a test version, not published on the Play Store.

### What the app does

- **Accompaniment Player**: a band (bass, piano, drums) plays any of 38,307 songs. Choose the style, key and tempo, loop a passage, or go through all twelve keys.
- **Name the progression**: hear a passage of a real song and recognise the chord progression.
- **Chord Sightreading**: connect a MIDI keyboard and play the chords of a song while bass and drums play along. Each chord turns green when you play it in time.

The songs come from the ChoCo collection of chord charts, checked and corrected for this app (details in the README).
