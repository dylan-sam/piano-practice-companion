First public version of Piano Practice Companion.

**Try it**

- **In the browser:** the web demo, linked at the top of the README (no install).
- **On Android:** download `piano-practice-companion-debug.apk` below, open it on the phone and allow installing it (Android 7.0 or newer).

**What's inside**

- **Accompaniment Player:** a band (bass, piano, drums) plays any of 38,307 chord charts in six styles, with practice loops, key cycling, tempo ramps, a mixer, Roman numerals, a Style lab, and iReal Pro import and export.
- **Name the progression:** ear training on real songs, in levels.
- **Chord Sightreading:** play the chords on a MIDI keyboard (USB or Bluetooth) while bass and drums play along; pick a song, a random song or an endless line of progressions.
- **Phones:** layouts for portrait and landscape, auto-scrolling sheets, the back button closes menus first.

The song catalog is built from ChoCo, checked and corrected (see the README). The APK is a debug build, not signed for the Play Store: Android may warn that it comes from an unknown developer.
